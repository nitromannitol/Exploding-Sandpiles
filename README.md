# Exploding-Sandpiles

A Lean 4 formalization of

> **A shape theorem for exploding sandpiles**
> Ahmed Bou-Rabee

Every theorem, proposition, lemma and corollary of the paper is transcribed as
an exact Lean statement with a line anchor into the pinned source, registered in
`ledger/manifest.yaml`, and then proved.  The bytes between
`-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-BEGIN` and `-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-END` in each file are the
contract; a proof may be written after the end marker and never inside.

Results the paper cites rather than proves enter as `Prop`s in
`Exploding/External/`, frozen and pinned in the same way, and are taken as
explicit hypotheses by the statements whose proofs use them; they are never
axioms.

## The model

A *background* is a function `η : ℤ^d → ℤ` counting chips at each site.  Add `n`
chips at the origin and topple in parallel: every site holding at least `2d`
chips fires, sending one chip to each neighbour.  The background is *robust* if
this halts for every `n`, and *explosive* otherwise.  For an explosive
background the paper studies the infinite sequence of odometers, and asks
whether the growing region of toppled sites, rescaled by time, has a limit
shape.

It does, for the Bernoulli background of `(1.2)` which holds `2d - 2` chips with
probability `1 - p` and `2d - 1` with probability `p`: the rescaled arrival
times converge to a continuous convex one-homogeneous function (Theorem 1.3)
and the support converges to a convex body (Theorem 1.2).  Section 5 gives
hypotheses under which this holds in general, Section 6 gives explosive
backgrounds with no limit shape at all, and Section 7 gives a criterion for a
background to be explosive (Theorem 1.4).

## Where to start reading

- `Exploding/Support/Toppling.lean` — parallel toppling, the frozen variant, the
  `n`-wave, and the notions of stabilizable, robust and explosive.
- `Exploding/Support/Vocab.lean` — boxes, faces, bounding rectangles, crossing
  times and good cubes, the last wave and its arrival times, recurrence, the
  background law.
- `Exploding/Support/Hypotheses.lean` — the five hypotheses of Section 5.
- `Exploding/Support/Reduction.lean` — the constrained processes of Section 7.1.
- `Exploding/Frozen/` — one frozen statement per file.
- `CORRESPONDENCE.md` — the map from each node to the paper.
