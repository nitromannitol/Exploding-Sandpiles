# Paper sources

- `exploding-arxiv.tex`: the arXiv v2 source of *A shape theorem for exploding sandpiles*, byte-identical to the e-print.
- `arxiv/`: the macros `defs.tex` and the bibliography `exploding_rand.bbl` of the arXiv v2 source.
- `defs.tex`: the macros, identical to `arxiv/defs.tex` and `corrected-arxiv/defs.tex`.
- `exploding.tex`: an earlier journal-format revision, superseded by the corrected edition. The two results the paper quotes (Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 7.4) and the proofs of the cited results keep their line citations to it, and the checks in `tools/` read it.
- `corrected-arxiv/`: the corrected edition `exploding-corrected-arxiv.tex`, with its section "Corrections to the published version", its `defs.tex`, bibliography, figures and `SOURCE_NOTE.md`.
