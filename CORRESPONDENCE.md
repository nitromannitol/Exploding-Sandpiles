# Correspondence: paper ↔ Lean

This file maps the statements of *A shape theorem for exploding sandpiles* (Ahmed Bou-Rabee) to
their Lean declarations. The paper is read from its corrected edition,
[`paper/corrected-arxiv/exploding-corrected-arxiv.tex`](paper/corrected-arxiv/exploding-corrected-arxiv.tex),
whose section "Corrections to the published version" lists every change from arXiv v2. The
statements are in `Exploding/Frozen/`, and `Exploding/MainTheorems.lean` restates the main results
with their cited inputs discharged where they are proved. The two tables at the end are generated
by `tools/sync_docs.py`; the rest of the file is written by hand.

All 22 numbered results of the corrected edition and its Remark 1 are proved in Lean, with no `sorry` and only the standard axioms, from three cited results that enter as explicit hypotheses; Theorem 6.1 is stated as published, Theorems 1.2 and 1.3 as published with the law (2) read as one common law at every site, and Theorems 1.4 and 5.1 with the corrections listed in the corrected edition; Theorem 5.1 also assumes Hypothesis 1 in a stronger form than printed, and whether it holds under the printed form is open.

## Conventions

- The statements are read from the corrected edition. Its SHA-256 and that of its `defs.tex` input are the source pins in [`ledger/manifest.yaml`](ledger/manifest.yaml).
- The bytes between `-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-BEGIN` and `-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-END` are the contract. A proof is written after the end marker and never changes the statement. `python3 tools/check_manifest.py` checks the hashes.
- A statement in `Exploding/Frozen/` keeps each cited input that it uses as an explicit binder. The restatement in `Exploding/MainTheorems.lean` supplies the proofs of the inputs that are proved in this repository.

## Cited results

## How a statement of the paper is read in Lean

| paper | Lean reading | why |
|---|---|---|
| every arrival time and the crossing time | valued in `ℕ∞` | `sInf ∅ = ⊤` there is the paper's `∞`; in `ℕ` it is the junk value `0` |
| the bounds on arrival times against real quantities | conjoined with `∃ m : ℕ, T = m` | carries the finiteness the paper asserts and avoids `ENat.toNat ⊤ = 0` |
| locally uniform convergence | written in its `ε`-form, not as a supremum | `sSup ∅ = 0` and an unbounded `sSup` is `0` in `ℝ` |
| `prop:renormalization_properties`, clause (2) | the finiteness of the component is conjoined with the cardinality bound | `Set.ncard` of an infinite set is `0` |
| `prop:renormalization_properties` (Proposition 2.2) | retains the three percolation inputs, states `0 < p ≤ 1`, and includes maximal-component existence and uniqueness of every infinite good-cube component | the statement is fixed by its source pin in the manifest |
| `lemma:local_convexity` | the finiteness of `br(A)` is carried, and `\|br(A)\|` read off it | same reason |
| `lemma:local_convexity`, `lemma:last_wave_bound`, `lemma:approx_subadditivity` | the standing hypotheses of Sections 2 and 3 (`2d - 2 ≤ η ≤ 2d - 1`, the last wave well defined) are explicit hypotheses | those lemmas assert nothing probabilistic; the section fixes `η` before stating them |
| `prop:dimensional_reduction` | the paper's `d` is written `d + 1` | so that the lower-dimensional lattice is `ℤ^d` rather than `ℤ^{d-1}` |
| `theorem:limit_shape` | "convex domain" is `Convex ℝ B ∧ IsOpen B ∧ B.Nonempty` | a nonempty open convex set is connected, hence a domain |
| the floor `⌊·⌋` in the toppling rules of Sections 2 and 3 | integer division `/` on `ℤ` | `/` on `ℤ` is `Int.ediv`, which rounds towards `-∞` |
| `lemma:square_lower_bound` | "the support of the `m`-wave" is `∃ t, 0 < u_t x` | the wave odometer is nondecreasing in time on a nonnegative background, so this is the support of the terminal odometer when the wave stabilizes, and is the right reading when it does not |
| the statements of Sections 1 to 4 | carry `2 ≤ d` | the standing dimension assumption of those sections; the Lean statements take it as an explicit binder |
| the seed of the frozen toppling of Section 2, in Lemma 2.1, Proposition 2.1, Hypotheses 2 to 4 and recurrence | `fodo S η w₀` evolves `s_t' = η + Δ w_t`, so its initial configuration is the delivered seed `η + Δ w₀` | this is the corrected edition's convention (correction C05 in the README); with the published `s_0' = η` nothing topples on an L-shaped path on `η ≡ 2d - 2` |
| the Bernoulli probability `p` of the law (2) | `0 < p ≤ 1` in `lemma:finite_exploding`, `prop:convergence_of_the_last_wave`, `lemma:last_penultimate_cluster`, `lemma:penultimate_upper_bound`, `lemma:last_penultimate_close`, `prop:approximate_subadditive`, `prop:renormalization_properties`, `theorem:limit_shape` and `theorem:convergence_of_time_constant` | the endpoint `p = 1` is included |

The Hypotheses of Section 5 are numbered 1 to 5 in the paper; Hypothesis 1 is the finite range of dependence.
